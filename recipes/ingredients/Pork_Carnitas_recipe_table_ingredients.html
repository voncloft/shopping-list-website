Ingredients:
Makes 7 generous meal-prep servings.
4 lb (1.81 kg) boneless pork shoulder / Boston butt, weighed after removing skin and the thick exterior fat cap; cut into 2-inch (5 cm) chunks
1 large yellow onion (200 g), diced
6 garlic cloves (18 g), minced
2¼ cups (540 ml) low-sodium chicken broth
½ cup (120 ml) orange juice
¼ cup (60 ml) lime juice
1 tbsp ground cumin (6 g)
2 tsp dried oregano (2 g)
1 tsp fine salt (6 g) and 1 tsp ground black pepper (2 g)
2 dried bay leaves; remove after cooking
1 tbsp (14 g) olive oil, for crisping the full batch
Allergens: Check broth labels. This recipe uses no avocado or avocado oil.

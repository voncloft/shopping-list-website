Ingredients:
Makes 7 servings. All amounts below are for the full batch.
3 1/2 lb boneless beef chuck roast, cut into 3-inch pieces
2 1/4 cups low-sodium beef broth
1 oz packet dry ranch seasoning (about 3 tablespoons)
4 oz drained pepperoncini peppers, stems removed, plus 1/4 cup brine from the jar
1 oz packet au jus gravy mix
4 tablespoons unsalted butter
1/4 cup cold tap water for the gravy mix, plus up to 1/2 cup if the meat needs longer simmering
Allergens: Ranch usually contains milk; gravy mix may contain wheat and soy. Check both packets.
This is the beef and gravy only. Rice, potatoes, or bread are not included in the shopping quantities.

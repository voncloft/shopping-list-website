Ingredients:
Makes 7 servings. All amounts below are for the full batch.
3 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, thawed
2 1/4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
2 teaspoons garlic powder
1 teaspoon onion powder
16 oz dry elbow macaroni
4 quarts tap water for boiling the macaroni
1/2 cup plain cayenne hot sauce
2 tablespoons unsalted butter
4 oz cream cheese, cubed
1 cup whole milk
16 oz cheddar cheese, freshly grated
Allergens: Milk and wheat. Check hot sauce and broth labels.
The macaroni cooks separately so it does not turn mushy while the chicken cooks. A stove pot, colander, and large mixing bowl are needed.

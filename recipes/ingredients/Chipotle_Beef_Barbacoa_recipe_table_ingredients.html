Ingredients:
Makes 7 generous meal-prep servings.
4 lb (1.81 kg) boneless beef chuck, weighed after removing hard exterior fat; cut into 3-inch (7.5 cm) chunks
1 tbsp (14 g) olive oil
1 large yellow onion (200 g), diced
6 garlic cloves (18 g), minced
2¼ cups (540 ml) low-sodium beef broth
¼ cup (60 ml) apple cider vinegar
3 canned chipotle peppers (30 g), chopped, plus 2 tbsp (30 g) adobo sauce
1 tbsp ground cumin (6 g)
2 tsp dried oregano (2 g)
1 tsp fine salt (6 g) and 1 tsp ground black pepper (2 g)
2 dried bay leaves; remove after cooking
¼ cup (60 ml) lime juice, for finishing
Allergens: Check broth and adobo labels for wheat, soy or other allergens.

Ingredients:
Makes 7 servings. All amounts below are for the full batch.
3 1/2 lb boneless pork shoulder, large outside fat trimmed, cut into 3-inch pieces
2 1/4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
1 cup frozen chopped onion
8 garlic cloves, finely chopped
1 1/2 teaspoons ground ginger (plain dried ginger powder)
1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce
1/3 cup gochujang Korean chili paste
3 tablespoons packed brown sugar
2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
1 tablespoon cornstarch
1 tablespoon cold tap water, plus up to 1/2 cup if pork needs more simmering
Allergens: Soy, sesame, and usually wheat. Check gochujang and soy sauce labels.
Spicy pork only; rice and other sides are not included.

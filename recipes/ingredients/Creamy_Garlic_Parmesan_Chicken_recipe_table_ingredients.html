Ingredients:
Makes 7 generous meal-prep servings.
4 lb (1.81 kg) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, thawed and unfolded
1 tbsp (14 g) olive oil
1 large yellow onion (200 g), diced
10 garlic cloves (30 g), minced
2¼ cups (540 ml) low-sodium chicken broth
2 tsp Italian seasoning (2 g)
½ tsp fine salt (3 g) and 1 tsp ground black pepper (2 g)
1 cup (240 ml) heavy cream
8 oz (227 g) full-fat block cream cheese, cubed
4 oz (113 g) Parmesan, finely grated by weight
5 oz (142 g) fresh spinach
1 tbsp (15 ml) lemon juice
Allergens: Contains milk. Check broth labels for additional allergens.

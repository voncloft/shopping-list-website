Ingredients:
Makes 7 servings. All amounts below are for the full batch.
3 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, thawed
1 tablespoon olive oil
1 cup frozen chopped onion
8 garlic cloves, finely chopped
2 1/4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
2 teaspoons Italian seasoning
4 oz sun-dried tomatoes, drained and chopped
1 cup heavy cream
4 oz finely grated Parmesan
5 oz fresh baby spinach
1 tablespoon lemon juice
1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
Allergens: Milk. Check broth and sun-dried tomato labels.

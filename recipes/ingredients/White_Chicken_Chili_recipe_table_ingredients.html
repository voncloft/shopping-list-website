Ingredients:
Makes 7 servings. All amounts below are for the full batch.
3 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, thawed
1 tablespoon olive oil
1 cup frozen chopped onion
6 garlic cloves, finely chopped
4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
2 cans (4 oz each) mild diced roasted green chiles, with their liquid
2 teaspoons ground cumin
1 teaspoon dried oregano
1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
3 cans (15 oz each) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
2 cups frozen corn
8 oz cream cheese, cut into small cubes
2 tablespoons lime juice
1/4 cup chopped cilantro
Allergens: Milk. Check broth labels.
Use canned, fully cooked beans. These directions do not cook dry beans. If the finished chili is too full to stir safely, combine and heat it in a larger stove pot.

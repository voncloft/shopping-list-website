Ingredients:
Makes 7 generous meal-prep servings.
3 lb (1.36 kg) raw 85%-lean ground beef
1 lb (454 g) raw ground pork
1 cup (80 g) plain dry breadcrumbs
½ cup (120 ml) whole milk
2 large eggs (100 g without shells)
3½ oz (100 g) Parmesan, finely grated by weight
6 garlic cloves (18 g), minced
¼ cup chopped parsley (15 g)
2 tsp Italian seasoning (2 g)
1 tsp fine salt (6 g) and 1 tsp ground black pepper (2 g)
2¼ cups (540 ml) water for an 8-qt batch; use this full amount for EACH of two 6-qt batches
Two 24-oz jars marinara sauce (48 oz / 1.36 kg total)
Allergens: Contains wheat, milk and egg. Check marinara labels for other allergens. Breadcrumbs mean this is not a strict keto recipe.

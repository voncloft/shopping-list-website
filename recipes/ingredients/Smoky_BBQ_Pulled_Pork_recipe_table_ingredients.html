Ingredients:
Makes 7 servings. All amounts below are for the full batch.
3 1/2 lb boneless pork shoulder, large outside fat trimmed, cut into 3-inch pieces
2 1/4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
1 cup frozen chopped onion
1 tablespoon smoked paprika
2 teaspoons garlic powder
1 teaspoon onion powder
1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
2 tablespoons packed brown sugar
2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
1 1/2 cups smoky barbecue sauce
Up to 1/2 cup tap water only if pork needs more simmering
Allergens: Check barbecue sauce and broth labels; sauces may contain soy, wheat, or fish.
Pulled pork only; buns and coleslaw are not included.

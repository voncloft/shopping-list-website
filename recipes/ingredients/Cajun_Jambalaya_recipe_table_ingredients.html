Ingredients:
Makes 7 servings. All amounts below are for the full batch.
2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1 1/2-inch pieces
1 lb fully cooked andouille sausage, cut into 1/4-inch slices
1 tablespoon olive oil
1 cup frozen chopped onion
2 medium bell peppers, seeded and chopped
3 medium celery stalks, chopped
6 garlic cloves, finely chopped
2 tablespoons low-salt Cajun seasoning
1 teaspoon smoked paprika
2 1/4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
1 can (14.5 oz) no-salt-added diced tomatoes, with the juice
2 bay leaves
2 cups dry long-grain white rice, not instant or brown rice
4 cups tap water for the rice, or the amount its package specifies for 2 cups dry rice
Allergens: Check sausage, Cajun seasoning, and broth labels; they may contain wheat, soy, or milk.
This tomato-containing version includes chicken, sausage, and rice. You need a stove saucepan with a lid and a large mixing bowl. Rice cooks separately to keep its texture predictable.

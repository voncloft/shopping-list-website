Ingredients:
Makes 7 servings. All amounts below are for the full batch.
3 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1 1/2-inch pieces
1 tablespoon olive oil
1 cup frozen chopped onion
8 garlic cloves, finely chopped
2 tablespoons peeled, finely chopped fresh ginger
2 1/4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
2 cans (14.5 oz each) no-salt-added diced tomatoes, with their juice
2 tablespoons tomato paste
2 tablespoons garam masala, divided
2 teaspoons sweet paprika
1 teaspoon ground turmeric
2 teaspoons ground cumin
1 teaspoon salt
1/2 cup heavy cream
1/2 cup plain whole-milk yogurt
1 tablespoon lemon juice
1/4 cup chopped cilantro
Allergens: Milk. Check spice blends and broth labels.
A simple pressure-cooker version. Rice or naan is not included.

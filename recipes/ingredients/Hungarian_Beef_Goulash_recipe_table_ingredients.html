Ingredients:
Makes 7 servings. All amounts below are for the full batch.
3 1/2 lb boneless beef chuck, cut into 1 1/2-inch cubes
1 tablespoon olive oil
2 cups frozen chopped onion
2 medium bell peppers, seeded and chopped
6 garlic cloves, finely chopped
2 1/4 cups low-sodium beef broth
3 tablespoons sweet Hungarian paprika
1 teaspoon caraway seeds
2 tablespoons tomato paste
1 can (14.5 oz) no-salt-added diced tomatoes, with the juice
1 teaspoon salt
1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
2 tablespoons cornstarch
2 tablespoons cold tap water for thickening, plus up to 1/2 cup if beef needs more simmering
1/2 cup sour cream, for serving
Allergens: Milk in sour cream. Check broth labels.
This is paprika beef stew. Noodles, potatoes, or bread are not included.

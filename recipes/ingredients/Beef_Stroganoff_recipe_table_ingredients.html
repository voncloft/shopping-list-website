Ingredients:
Makes 7 generous meal-prep servings.
4 lb (1.81 kg) boneless beef chuck, trimmed of hard exterior fat; cut into 1½-inch (4 cm) cubes
1 tbsp (14 g) olive oil
2 tbsp (28 g) unsalted butter
1½ lb (680 g) mushrooms, sliced
1 large yellow onion (200 g), diced
4 garlic cloves (12 g), minced
2¼ cups (540 ml) low-sodium beef broth
2 tbsp (30 ml) Worcestershire sauce
2 tbsp (30 g) Dijon mustard
1 tsp fine salt (6 g) and 1 tsp ground black pepper (2 g)
3 tbsp cornstarch (24 g), mixed with 3 tbsp cold water (45 ml), for the finish
1½ cups (360 g) full-fat sour cream
Allergens: Contains milk. Worcestershire often contains fish (anchovies); check for wheat and soy too. Optional egg noodles contain wheat and egg.

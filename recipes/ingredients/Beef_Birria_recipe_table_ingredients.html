Ingredients:
Makes 7 servings. All amounts below are for the full batch.
3 1/2 lb boneless beef chuck, cut into 3-inch pieces
6 dried guajillo peppers, stems and seeds removed
2 dried ancho peppers, stems and seeds removed
2 cups cold tap water brought to a boil for soaking peppers, plus up to 1/2 cup if the beef needs more simmering
3 1/4 cups low-sodium beef broth, divided
1 cup frozen chopped onion
8 garlic cloves, peeled
1 can (14.5 oz) no-salt-added diced tomatoes, with the juice
2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
2 teaspoons ground cumin
2 teaspoons dried oregano
1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
1 cinnamon stick
2 bay leaves
1/2 teaspoon salt
2 tablespoons lime juice
1/4 cup chopped cilantro
Allergens: Check the broth label.
This is birria beef with broth, not a taco kit. Tortillas and cheese are not included. You need a blender and a kettle or stove saucepan.

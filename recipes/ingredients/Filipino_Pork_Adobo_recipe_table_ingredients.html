Ingredients:
Makes 7 servings. All amounts below are for the full batch.
3 1/2 lb boneless pork shoulder, large outside fat trimmed, cut into 1 1/2-inch cubes
2 1/4 cups tap water, plus up to 1/2 cup if pork needs more simmering
1/2 cup low-sodium soy sauce
1/2 cup white vinegar
12 garlic cloves, peeled and lightly crushed
3 bay leaves
1 teaspoon ground black pepper
1 tablespoon packed brown sugar
Allergens: Soy and usually wheat in soy sauce.
This recipe uses shoulder rather than pork belly. Rice is not included.

Ingredients:
Makes 7 servings. All amounts below are for the full batch.
4 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, thawed
1 tablespoon olive oil
1 cup frozen chopped onion
2 medium bell peppers, seeds removed and cut into strips
6 garlic cloves, finely chopped
4 tablespoons low-salt Cajun seasoning
2 1/4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
1 cup heavy cream
8 oz cream cheese, cut into small cubes
3/4 cup finely grated Parmesan
5 Jalapenos
Allergens: Milk. Check Cajun seasoning and broth labels.

Steps:
Creamy Garlic Parmesan Chicken
High pressure: 10 minutes. Wait after cooking: 10 minutes with the lid closed, then release the remaining steam as explained below.
Approximate total time: 55–75 min. All meat must be thawed.
Use a 6- or 8-quart Instant Pot. Stay below the line marked PC MAX inside the metal pot, about two-thirds full. The amounts in the steps are for one full batch. If the full recipe will not fit, cook two half batches. Use half of each amount listed in the steps, except the broth added before pressure cooking: use 2¼ cups of that broth in EACH batch. Keep the pressure-cooking time the same. Buy extra broth for two batches. A 3-quart pot is too small for these directions.
Never fill above the PC MAX line. Follow your model manual for its minimum liquid and its steam-release controls.
In these directions, "pot" means the removable metal pot inside the Instant Pot. Keep the lid off until a step tells you to close it. Have a food thermometer, a large spoon, tongs, and a clean plate or bowl ready. Use thawed meat. Chop or slice the ingredients as listed before turning on the pot.

1). Cook the onion
Leave the lid off. Select Sauté / Normal. Add 1 tablespoon olive oil and the diced onion to the Instant Pot. Stir and cook for 4 minutes.

2). Add the garlic
Add the 10 finely chopped garlic cloves to the onion in the pot. Stir for 30 seconds.

3). Add broth and loosen the browned bits
Press Cancel. Pour 2¼ cups chicken broth into the Instant Pot. Scrape the bottom with a wooden spoon until no browned bits are stuck there.

4). Season the broth
Stir 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning, ½ teaspoon salt, and 1 teaspoon black pepper into the broth in the pot.

5). Put the raw chicken into the broth
Add all 4 pounds of thawed chicken thighs to the Instant Pot. Unfold any rolled-up pieces and arrange them in loose layers. Stay below the pressure-cooking maximum line. Keep the cream, cheeses, spinach, and lemon juice out of the pot for now.

6). Close the lid
Put the lid on the Instant Pot and turn it until it locks. If your model has a Sealing/Venting switch, move it to Sealing. Follow your model's instructions if its controls look different.

7). Cook on High pressure for 10 minutes
Press Pressure Cook. Choose High pressure and set the cooking time to 10 minutes. Press Start if your model requires it. The pot may take 15–25 minutes to build pressure before the cooking countdown starts.

8). Wait 10 minutes after cooking ends
When pressure cooking finishes, start a separate 10-minute timer. Leave the lid closed and do not touch the steam-release control during this wait. This lets some pressure drop by itself.

9). Let the remaining steam out
After the waiting time, use your model's steam-release control to release the remaining pressure. Keep your face and hands away from the steam. If liquid sprays out, stop releasing steam and wait longer before trying again.

10). Open the lid
Wait until steam stops coming out and the small pressure pin has dropped. Unlock the lid and lift it so the steam goes away from your face. Never force the lid open.

11). Check that the chicken is cooked
Put a food thermometer into the middle of several of the thickest chicken pieces. Each must read at least 165°F. If any piece is cooler, leave the lid off, select Sauté, and let the chicken cook in the bubbling broth. Check again until the thick pieces reach 165°F.

12). Move the cooked chicken to a clean plate
Use tongs to lift every chicken piece out of the Instant Pot and onto a clean plate. Leave the cooking liquid in the pot. Keep the plate of chicken nearby; the chicken will go back into the sauce later.

13). Save 1 cup of the cooking liquid
Use a ladle to put exactly 1 cup of the hot liquid from the Instant Pot into a heatproof measuring cup. Set this cup aside. This is the liquid you will use to make the sauce.

14). Remove the extra liquid
Ladle the remaining liquid out of the Instant Pot into a separate heatproof bowl. Leave the cooked onion pieces in the pot if possible. Set the bowl aside to cool before discarding that extra liquid.

15). Put the saved cup of liquid back into the pot
Pour the 1 cup of cooking liquid from the measuring cup back into the Instant Pot. Select Sauté / Low and leave the lid off.

16). Melt the cream cheese in the pot
Add the entire 8-ounce block of cream cheese, cut into small cubes, to the liquid in the Instant Pot. Stir or whisk until the cubes melt and the mixture is smooth.

17). Add the heavy cream
Pour 1 cup heavy cream into the Instant Pot. Stir and warm gently for 3–5 minutes. Keep the heat low; do not let the mixture boil hard.

18). Stir in the Parmesan
Press Cancel. Add the 4 ounces of finely grated Parmesan to the sauce in the Instant Pot a little at a time. Stir after each addition until the cheese melts.

19). Add the spinach
Add all 5 ounces of spinach to the sauce in the Instant Pot. Stir until the leaves become soft and shrink down.

20). Add the lemon juice
Stir 1 tablespoon lemon juice into the sauce and spinach in the Instant Pot.

21). Cut or shred the cooked chicken
On the clean plate, cut the cooked chicken into bite-size pieces or pull it into strips with two forks. Keep any liquid that collects on the plate.

22). Put the chicken back into the sauce
Move all the chicken from the plate into the Instant Pot with the creamy sauce and spinach. Pour in the juices from the plate. Stir until the sauce coats the chicken.

23). Warm the chicken and sauce together
Check the chicken with a food thermometer. If it has cooled below 165°F, select Sauté / Low and stir gently until the chicken and sauce reach 165°F. Avoid a hard boil. Press Cancel when finished.

24). Fill seven containers
Put out seven shallow food containers. Divide the cooked meat and all its sauce evenly among them. One container is one meal. Follow the cooling and storage directions below right away.

Food safety
Chicken must reach 165°F (74°C) in its thickest parts. Check multiple pieces with a food thermometer; appearance and pressure time alone do not confirm doneness.
Wash hands and tools after handling raw meat; keep cooked food off raw-meat plates. Do not rinse raw poultry.

Meal-prep storage
Divide promptly into shallow containers; refrigerate within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C). Do not leave the full pot out to cool. Keep the fridge at 40°F / 4°C or colder. Keep portions 1-3 refrigerated and freeze portions 4-7 on cooking day. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3-4 days. Freeze at 0°F / -18°C or colder.

Freezing and reheating
Freeze the fully cooked chicken and sauce together; best texture within 2 months. Cream sauce may separate after freezing. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator, then reheat gently while stirring; a splash of water helps bring the sauce together.
Reheat to 165°F / 74°C throughout. In a microwave, cover with a vented lid, stir or turn, let stand briefly and check several spots with a thermometer. Do not thaw on the counter or use Keep Warm to reheat. Freezer times above are quality targets.

Pork Chile Verde
High pressure: 45 minutes. Wait after cooking: 15 minutes with the lid closed, then release the remaining steam as explained below.
Approximate total time: 1 hr 30 min–1 hr 50 min. All meat must be thawed.
Use a 6- or 8-quart Instant Pot. Stay below the line marked PC MAX inside the metal pot, about two-thirds full. The amounts in the steps are for one full batch. If the full recipe will not fit, cook two half batches. Use half of each amount listed in the steps, except the broth added before pressure cooking: use 2¼ cups of that broth in EACH batch. Keep the pressure-cooking time the same. Buy extra broth for two batches. A 3-quart pot is too small for these directions.
Never fill above the PC MAX line. Follow your model manual for its minimum liquid and its steam-release controls.
In these directions, "pot" means the removable metal pot inside the Instant Pot. Keep the lid off until a step tells you to close it. Have a food thermometer, a large spoon, tongs, and a clean plate or bowl ready. Use thawed meat. Chop or slice the ingredients as listed before turning on the pot.

1). Get the pork ready
Use 4 pounds of thawed, boneless pork shoulder after trimming off the skin and thick outer layer of fat. Cut the meat into chunks about 2 inches across. Sprinkle with ½ teaspoon salt and ½ teaspoon black pepper.

2). Brown the first batch of pork
Leave the lid off. Select Sauté / High and add 1 tablespoon olive oil to the Instant Pot. Add about one-third of the pork in one layer. Brown it for about 2 minutes on each broad side. Move it to a plate.

3). Brown the remaining pork
Brown the rest of the pork in two more batches the same way. Put every batch on the plate. All the pork should now be outside the pot. It is still raw in the middle.

4). Cook the onion
Add the diced onion to the Instant Pot, leaving the oil and browned bits in the pot. Stir and cook for 3 minutes.

5). Add the garlic
Add the 6 finely chopped garlic cloves to the onion in the Instant Pot. Stir for 30 seconds.

6). Add broth and loosen the browned bits
Press Cancel. Pour 2¼ cups chicken broth into the Instant Pot. Scrape the bottom with a wooden spoon until all the stuck browned bits come loose and the bottom feels smooth.

7). Add cumin and oregano
Stir 2 teaspoons cumin and 1 teaspoon oregano into the broth in the Instant Pot.

8). Put the pork back into the pot
Move all the pork from the plate into the broth in the Instant Pot. Pour in any juices left on the plate. Stay below the pressure-cooking maximum line. Keep the salsa verde, canned green chiles, lime juice, and cilantro out for now.

9). Close the lid
Put the lid on the Instant Pot and turn it until it locks. If your model has a Sealing/Venting switch, move it to Sealing. Follow your model's instructions if its controls look different.

10). Cook on High pressure for 45 minutes
Press Pressure Cook. Choose High pressure and set the cooking time to 45 minutes. Press Start if your model requires it. The pot may take 15–25 minutes to build pressure before the cooking countdown starts.

11). Wait 15 minutes after cooking ends
When pressure cooking finishes, start a separate 15-minute timer. Leave the lid closed and do not touch the steam-release control during this wait. This lets some pressure drop by itself.

12). Let the remaining steam out
After the waiting time, use your model's steam-release control to release the remaining pressure. Keep your face and hands away from the steam. If liquid sprays out, stop releasing steam and wait longer before trying again.

13). Open the lid
Wait until steam stops coming out and the small pressure pin has dropped. Unlock the lid and lift it so the steam goes away from your face. Never force the lid open.

14). Check whether the pork is tender
Try cutting a pork chunk with the side of a fork. It should break apart easily. If it is still chewy, leave it in the broth, pressure cook for another 10 minutes on High, wait 10 minutes, then release the remaining steam and open the lid. Check again.

15). Move the cooked pork to a clean bowl
Use tongs to lift the cooked pork from the Instant Pot into a clean bowl. Leave the cooking liquid in the pot. Do not reuse the unwashed plate that held partly raw pork.

16). Cook down the liquid
Leave the lid off and select Sauté. Let the cooking liquid in the Instant Pot bubble for 8–15 minutes, stirring often. Some water will evaporate, leaving a little less liquid.

17). Add the green sauces to the pot
Stir 24 ounces of salsa verde into the liquid in the Instant Pot. Add both 4-ounce cans of diced green chiles, including the liquid in the cans. Use salsa verde without avocado.

18). Put the pork into the green sauce
Move the cooked pork from the bowl back into the Instant Pot. Pour in any juices left in the bowl. Stir gently so the green sauce coats the pork.

19). Let the sauce thicken
Keep the lid off and let the pork and green sauce bubble gently for 5–8 minutes. Stir often so the sauce does not stick to the bottom. Press Cancel when the sauce is as thick as you like.

20). Add lime juice and cilantro
Stir ¼ cup lime juice and ½ cup loosely packed chopped cilantro into the pork and green sauce in the Instant Pot. Leave the pork in chunks, or pull it into smaller pieces with two forks.

21). Fill seven containers
Put out seven shallow food containers. Divide the cooked meat and all its sauce evenly among them. One container is one meal. Follow the cooling and storage directions below right away.

Food safety
Whole-cut pork needs at least 145°F (63°C) with a 3-minute rest. Shoulder should also be fork-tender. Check salsa labels carefully if avoiding avocado for an allergy.
Wash hands and tools after handling raw meat; keep cooked food off raw-meat plates. Do not rinse raw poultry.

Meal-prep storage
Divide promptly into shallow containers; refrigerate within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C). Do not leave the full pot out to cool. Keep the fridge at 40°F / 4°C or colder. Keep portions 1-3 refrigerated and freeze portions 4-7 on cooking day. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3-4 days. Freeze at 0°F / -18°C or colder.

Freezing and reheating
Freeze pork and green sauce together; best texture within 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator. Reheat covered in a saucepan or microwave, stirring, until the pork and sauce reach 165°F throughout.
Reheat to 165°F / 74°C throughout. In a microwave, cover with a vented lid, stir or turn, let stand briefly and check several spots with a thermometer. Do not thaw on the counter or use Keep Warm to reheat. Freezer times above are quality targets.

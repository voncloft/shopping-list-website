Steps:
Italian Meatballs in Marinara
High pressure: 10 minutes. Wait after cooking: 10 minutes with the lid closed, then release the remaining steam as explained below.
Approximate total time: 65–85 min (8-qt). All meat must be thawed.
Use an 8-quart Instant Pot for all 42 meatballs if they fit below the pressure-cooking maximum line. For a 6-quart pot, or if the full batch will not fit, cook two batches of 21 meatballs. Use a fresh 2¼ cups water for EACH batch. A 3-quart pot is too small for these directions.
Never fill above the PC MAX line. Follow your model manual for its minimum liquid and its steam-release controls.
In these directions, "pot" means the removable metal pot inside the Instant Pot. Keep the lid off until a step tells you to close it. Have a food thermometer, a large spoon, tongs, and a clean plate or bowl ready. Use thawed meat. Chop or slice the ingredients as listed before turning on the pot.

1). Soak the breadcrumbs
Put 1 cup plain dry breadcrumbs and ½ cup whole milk into a large mixing bowl. Stir and let the mixture sit for 5 minutes.

2). Add eggs, cheese, and seasonings
To the breadcrumb bowl, add 2 eggs without their shells, 3½ ounces finely grated Parmesan, 6 finely chopped garlic cloves, ¼ cup chopped parsley, 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning, 1 teaspoon salt, and 1 teaspoon black pepper. Mix everything together.

3). Mix in the raw meat
Add 3 pounds thawed ground beef and 1 pound thawed ground pork to the mixing bowl. Use clean hands or a sturdy spoon to mix gently until the ingredients are spread evenly through the meat. Do not taste the raw mixture.

4). Make 42 meatballs
Divide the meat mixture into 42 equal pieces. Roll each piece into a ball about 1¾ inches across, roughly golf-ball size. Keep the meatballs refrigerated until the Instant Pot is ready. Wash your hands after touching raw meat.

5). Choose how many meatballs to cook at once
For an 8-quart Instant Pot, cook all 42 meatballs together if they fit below the pressure-cooking maximum line. For a 6-quart pot, cook 21 at a time and keep the other 21 in the refrigerator. Each batch uses a fresh 2¼ cups water.

6). Pour water into the pot
Pour 2¼ cups water into the Instant Pot. Do this for each batch, even when you are only cooking 21 meatballs.

7). Put the raw meatballs into the water
Gently place the meatballs in the Instant Pot in loose layers. Do not squash them together. Stay below the pressure-cooking maximum line. Keep both jars of marinara sauce out of the pot for now.

8). Close the lid
Put the lid on the Instant Pot and turn it until it locks. If your model has a Sealing/Venting switch, move it to Sealing. Follow your model's instructions if its controls look different.

9). Cook on High pressure for 10 minutes
Press Pressure Cook. Choose High pressure and set the cooking time to 10 minutes. Press Start if your model requires it. The pot may take 15–25 minutes to build pressure before the cooking countdown starts.

10). Wait 10 minutes after cooking ends
When pressure cooking finishes, start a separate 10-minute timer. Leave the lid closed and do not touch the steam-release control during this wait. This lets some pressure drop by itself.

11). Let the remaining steam out
After the waiting time, use your model's steam-release control to release the remaining pressure. Keep your face and hands away from the steam. If liquid sprays out, stop releasing steam and wait longer before trying again.

12). Open the lid
Wait until steam stops coming out and the small pressure pin has dropped. Unlock the lid and lift it so the steam goes away from your face. Never force the lid open.

13). Check that the meatballs are cooked
Put a food thermometer into the centers of several meatballs, including the largest ones and some from different layers. Each must reach at least 160°F. If any are cooler, leave the lid off, select Sauté, and cook them in the bubbling liquid until their centers reach 160°F.

14). Move the cooked meatballs to a clean bowl
Press Cancel. Use a spoon with holes to lift the cooked meatballs from the Instant Pot into a clean, large bowl. Leave the cooking water in the pot for now.

15). Cook the second batch if needed
If you still have 21 raw meatballs in the refrigerator, ladle the first batch's cooking water into a heatproof bowl and set it aside to cool. Repeat steps 6–14 with the remaining 21 raw meatballs, starting with a fresh 2¼ cups water. Keep the first cooked batch at 140°F or hotter in a covered saucepan while the second batch cooks.

16). Remove the cooking water
Once all the meatballs are cooked and out of the Instant Pot, ladle the remaining cooking water into a heatproof bowl. Let it cool before discarding it. The Instant Pot should now be empty.

17). Warm the marinara sauce
Pour both 24-ounce jars of marinara sauce into the empty Instant Pot. Leave the lid off and select Sauté / Low. Stir until the sauce starts bubbling gently. If all the sauce and meatballs will not fit, use a large saucepan on the stove for this step and the next two steps.

18). Put the cooked meatballs into the marinara
Gently move all 42 cooked meatballs from their bowl or saucepan into the warm marinara sauce. Use a spoon to coat the meatballs with sauce without breaking them.

19). Heat the meatballs and sauce together
Cook uncovered at a gentle bubble for 5–10 minutes. Stir carefully now and then. If the cooked meatballs have cooled, continue heating until their centers reach 165°F. Turn off the heat when finished.

20). Fill seven containers
Put 6 meatballs into each of seven shallow containers. Divide all the marinara sauce equally among the containers. Keep any pasta separate. Follow the cooling and storage directions below right away.

Food safety
Ground beef and pork must reach 160°F (71°C) in the center of each meatball. Do not use the 145°F whole-cut-meat rule. Reheated cooked meatballs need 165°F (74°C).
Wash hands and tools after handling raw meat; keep cooked food off raw-meat plates. Do not rinse raw poultry.

Meal-prep storage
Divide promptly into shallow containers; refrigerate within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C). Do not leave the full pot out to cool. Keep the fridge at 40°F / 4°C or colder. Keep portions 1-3 refrigerated and freeze portions 4-7 on cooking day. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3-4 days. Freeze at 0°F / -18°C or colder.

Freezing and reheating
Freeze six meatballs per portion in their share of sauce; best texture within 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator. Reheat covered in a saucepan or microwave, turning gently, until the centers of several meatballs reach 165°F.
Reheat to 165°F / 74°C throughout. In a microwave, cover with a vented lid, stir or turn, let stand briefly and check several spots with a thermometer. Do not thaw on the counter or use Keep Warm to reheat. Freezer times above are quality targets.

Recipe nutrition estimate
About 840 kcal and 59 g protein per one-seventh of the finished recipe, including sauce, excluding sides. Allow roughly 20% variation in calories and 15% in protein, sometimes more for fatty meat. These recipe estimates use generic ingredients; your app calculates from its item records, which can differ. Assumes 85%-lean beef, regular ground pork and marinara at about 60 kcal per 100 g. Counts all meat fat before cooking; discarding the cooking liquid will usually reduce actual calories. Six meatballs plus sauce is one serving.
Optional serving suggestion: Cook 14 oz (397 g) dry pasta separately for the batch: 2 oz (57 g) dry per serving, about +200 kcal and +7 g protein. That makes roughly 1040 kcal per pasta-and-meatball serving. Extra Parmesan is additional.

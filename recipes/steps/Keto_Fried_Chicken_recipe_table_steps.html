Steps:
1. Prepare the oven: Heat to 400°F and cover a large baking tray with parchment paper. Dry the chicken with paper towels, then add salt and pepper.
2. Prepare the egg wash: Beat the eggs and heavy cream together in a shallow dish.
3. Make the breading: In a separate dish, mix the almond flour, crushed pork rinds, Parmesan, garlic powder and paprika. Season lightly.
4. Coat the chicken: Dip each breast into the egg wash, then cover thoroughly with the dry breading. Press the crumbs against the chicken so they stick.
5. Bake: Arrange the chicken on the tray and cook for roughly 45 minutes, until golden and the thickest part of the meat registers at least 165°F.
6. Make the sauce: Blend the mayonnaise with the hot sauce, adjusting the heat to your preference. Serve alongside the chicken.

Chicken Tikka Masala
High pressure 8 minutes; wait 10 minutes before releasing steam.
Use a 6- or 8-quart Instant Pot. Before closing the lid, keep the food and liquid below the PC MAX / 2/3 line. This full batch is too large for a 3-quart pot. Follow your model manual if it requires more liquid. Never force an overfilled batch into the cooker.
Use thawed meat. Cut and measure the ingredients as listed before starting. In these steps, "pot" means the removable metal pot inside the Instant Pot. Keep the lid off except during the pressure-cooking steps. Wash hands and tools after handling raw meat; use a clean plate for cooked meat.
Allow extra time for preparation, pressure to build (about 15–25 minutes), and finishing the sauce.

1). With the lid off, select Sauté. Add the oil and onion. Stir for 4 minutes. Add the garlic and ginger; stir for 30 seconds. Press Cancel.

2). Pour in the broth and scrape the bottom clean with a wooden spoon. Stir in 1 tablespoon garam masala, all the paprika, turmeric, cumin, and salt.

3). Put the chicken pieces into the broth. Spoon the diced tomatoes and their juice and the tomato paste over the chicken. Leave them on top without stirring. Keep the cream, yogurt, lemon juice, cilantro, and remaining garam masala out for now.

4). Lock the lid. Set the steam control to Sealing if your model has that switch. Select Pressure Cook / High for 8 minutes. Press Start if needed. The cooking countdown begins after pressure builds.

5). When cooking ends, leave the lid closed for 10 minutes. Then use your model's steam-release control. Keep your hands and face away from the steam. If liquid sprays, stop releasing and wait longer.

6). Wait until all steam stops and the pressure pin drops. Open the lid away from your face. Never force the lid open.

7). Check several thick chicken pieces with a food thermometer. They must reach 165°F. If any are cooler, select Sauté and simmer the chicken in the liquid with the lid off until they reach 165°F.

8). Use a slotted spoon to lift the chicken onto a clean plate. Leave the tomatoes and liquid in the Instant Pot.

9). Select Sauté. Simmer the sauce with the lid off for 10–20 minutes, stirring often, until it is thick enough to lightly coat a spoon. Press Cancel.

10). Stir the heavy cream and remaining 1 tablespoon garam masala into the sauce. Let the bubbling stop.

11). Put the yogurt in a small bowl. Slowly stir 1/2 cup hot sauce from the Instant Pot into the yogurt. Pour that warmed yogurt mixture back into the Instant Pot and stir until smooth.

12). Put the chicken and plate juices back into the Instant Pot with the sauce. Stir in the lemon juice and cilantro. Warm gently on Sauté / Low if needed; avoid a hard boil. Press Cancel.

13). Divide the finished food and sauce evenly among 7 shallow containers. Each container is 1 serving. Follow the storage directions below.

Check doneness: Chicken must reach 165°F in the thickest parts. Use a food thermometer; cooking time alone is not a doneness check.
Store your 7 meals: Refrigerate in shallow containers within 2 hours (1 hour if the room is above 90°F). Keep the fridge at 40°F or colder. Keep the first 3 portions in the fridge and freeze the other 4 on cooking day. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3–4 days. Thaw frozen portions in the fridge.
Reheat: Heat each meal to 165°F throughout. In the microwave, use a vented cover, stir halfway through, let it stand briefly, and check several spots with a thermometer. Add a little water if needed.
Recipe developed for this collection; not kitchen-tested. Food safety: safe cooking temperatures, leftover storage, reheating. Cooker guidance: Instant Pot.

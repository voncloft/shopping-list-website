Filipino Pork Adobo
High pressure 25 minutes; wait 15 minutes before releasing steam.
Use a 6- or 8-quart Instant Pot. Before closing the lid, keep the food and liquid below the PC MAX / 2/3 line. This full batch is too large for a 3-quart pot. Follow your model manual if it requires more liquid. Never force an overfilled batch into the cooker.
Use thawed meat. Cut and measure the ingredients as listed before starting. In these steps, "pot" means the removable metal pot inside the Instant Pot. Keep the lid off except during the pressure-cooking steps. Wash hands and tools after handling raw meat; use a clean plate for cooked meat.
Allow extra time for preparation, pressure to build (about 15–25 minutes), and finishing the sauce.

1). Pour 2 1/4 cups water, the soy sauce, and vinegar into the Instant Pot. Add the crushed garlic, bay leaves, and black pepper.

2). Put the pork cubes into the liquid and stir once. Keep the brown sugar out for now.

3). Lock the lid. Set the steam control to Sealing if your model has that switch. Select Pressure Cook / High for 25 minutes. Press Start if needed. The cooking countdown begins after pressure builds.

4). When cooking ends, leave the lid closed for 15 minutes. Then use your model's steam-release control. Keep your hands and face away from the steam. If liquid sprays, stop releasing and wait longer.

5). Wait until all steam stops and the pressure pin drops. Open the lid away from your face. Never force the lid open.

6). Test a pork cube with a fork. If it is still chewy, select Sauté / Low and simmer uncovered for 10–20 more minutes. Add up to 1/2 cup water if needed to keep enough liquid in the pot.

7). Use a slotted spoon to move the pork and garlic onto a clean plate. Remove and discard all 3 bay leaves. Leave the cooking liquid in the Instant Pot.

8). Select Sauté. Boil the liquid with the lid off for 15–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until about 1 1/2 cups remain. Do not let the pot boil dry.

9). Stir in the brown sugar. Put the pork, garlic, and plate juices back into the Instant Pot with the sauce. Stir for 2 minutes to coat the pork. Press Cancel.

10). Divide the finished food and sauce evenly among 7 shallow containers. Each container is 1 serving. Follow the storage directions below.

Check doneness: Whole-cut beef and pork must reach at least 145°F and rest 3 minutes before eating. These shoulder and chuck recipes need longer cooking until fork-tender; do not stop at the minimum temperature if the meat is still tough.
Store your 7 meals: Refrigerate in shallow containers within 2 hours (1 hour if the room is above 90°F). Keep the fridge at 40°F or colder. Keep the first 3 portions in the fridge and freeze the other 4 on cooking day. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3–4 days. Thaw frozen portions in the fridge.
Reheat: Heat each meal to 165°F throughout. In the microwave, use a vented cover, stir halfway through, let it stand briefly, and check several spots with a thermometer. Add a little water if needed.
Recipe developed for this collection; not kitchen-tested. Food safety: safe cooking temperatures, leftover storage, reheating. Cooker guidance: Instant Pot.

Buffalo Chicken Mac and Cheese
Chicken: High pressure 10 minutes; wait 10 minutes. Cook macaroni on the stove.
Use a 6- or 8-quart Instant Pot. Before closing the lid, keep the food and liquid below the PC MAX / 2/3 line. This full batch is too large for a 3-quart pot. Follow your model manual if it requires more liquid. Never force an overfilled batch into the cooker.
Use thawed meat. Cut and measure the ingredients as listed before starting. In these steps, "pot" means the removable metal pot inside the Instant Pot. Keep the lid off except during the pressure-cooking steps. Wash hands and tools after handling raw meat; use a clean plate for cooked meat.
Allow extra time for preparation, pressure to build (about 15–25 minutes), and finishing the sauce.

1). Pour the broth into the Instant Pot. Stir in the garlic powder and onion powder. Unfold the chicken thighs and place them in the broth. Keep the pasta and all sauce ingredients out of the pressure cooker for now.

2). Lock the lid. Set the steam control to Sealing if your model has that switch. Select Pressure Cook / High for 10 minutes. Press Start if needed. The cooking countdown begins after pressure builds.

3). When cooking ends, leave the lid closed for 10 minutes. Then use your model's steam-release control. Keep your hands and face away from the steam. If liquid sprays, stop releasing and wait longer.

4). Wait until all steam stops and the pressure pin drops. Open the lid away from your face. Never force the lid open.

5). Check several thick chicken pieces with a food thermometer. They must reach 165°F. If any are cooler, select Sauté and simmer the chicken in the liquid with the lid off until they reach 165°F.

6). Move the cooked meat onto a clean plate. Pull it apart with two forks. Leave the cooking liquid in the Instant Pot.

7). Bring 4 quarts water to a boil in a large stove pot. Add the dry macaroni. Cook uncovered for the time on its box, stirring often. Drain in a colander. Put the drained macaroni in a large heatproof mixing bowl.

8). Save 1/2 cup of the chicken cooking liquid in a heatproof measuring cup. Ladle the rest into a heatproof bowl to cool before discarding.

9). Put the saved 1/2 cup liquid, butter, cream cheese, and milk in the Instant Pot. Select Sauté / Low with the lid off. Stir until the cream cheese melts. Do not boil hard.

10). Press Cancel. Stir in the cheddar a handful at a time until smooth. Stir in all 1/2 cup hot sauce.

11). Pour the cheese sauce into the large bowl of drained macaroni. Add the shredded chicken and plate juices. Stir until the sauce coats everything.

12). Divide the finished food and sauce evenly among 7 shallow containers. Each container is 1 serving. Follow the storage directions below.

Check doneness: Chicken must reach 165°F in the thickest parts. Use a food thermometer; cooking time alone is not a doneness check.
Store your 7 meals: Refrigerate in shallow containers within 2 hours (1 hour if the room is above 90°F). Keep the fridge at 40°F or colder. Keep the first 3 portions in the fridge and freeze the other 4 on cooking day. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3–4 days. Thaw frozen portions in the fridge.
Reheat: Heat each meal to 165°F throughout. In the microwave, use a vented cover, stir halfway through, let it stand briefly, and check several spots with a thermometer. Add a little water if needed.
Recipe developed for this collection; not kitchen-tested. Food safety: safe cooking temperatures, leftover storage, reheating. Cooker guidance: Instant Pot.

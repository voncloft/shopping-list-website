Creamy Cajun Chicken
High pressure 10 minutes; wait 10 minutes before releasing steam.
Use a 6- or 8-quart Instant Pot. Before closing the lid, keep the food and liquid below the PC MAX / 2/3 line. This full batch is too large for a 3-quart pot. Follow your model manual if it requires more liquid. Never force an overfilled batch into the cooker.
Use thawed meat. Cut and measure the ingredients as listed before starting. In these steps, "pot" means the removable metal pot inside the Instant Pot. Keep the lid off except during the pressure-cooking steps. Wash hands and tools after handling raw meat; use a clean plate for cooked meat.
Allow extra time for preparation, pressure to build (about 15–25 minutes), and finishing the sauce.

1). Put the metal pot in the Instant Pot. Leave the lid off. Select Sauté. Add the oil, onion, jalapenos (chopped), and bell pepper strips. Stir for 5 minutes.

2). Add all 6 chopped garlic cloves. Stir for 30 seconds. Press Cancel.

3). Pour in all 2 1/4 cups broth. Scrape the bottom with a wooden spoon until nothing is stuck. Stir in the Cajun seasoning.

4). Unfold the chicken thighs and place them in the broth. Keep the cream and cheeses out for now.

5). Lock the lid. Set the steam control to Sealing if your model has that switch. Select Pressure Cook / High for 10 minutes. Press Start if needed. The cooking countdown begins after pressure builds.

6). When cooking ends, leave the lid closed for 10 minutes. Then use your model's steam-release control. Keep your hands and face away from the steam. If liquid sprays, stop releasing and wait longer.

7). Wait until all steam stops and the pressure pin drops. Open the lid away from your face. Never force the lid open.

8). Check several thick chicken pieces with a food thermometer. They must reach 165°F. If any are cooler, select Sauté and simmer the chicken in the liquid with the lid off until they reach 165°F.

9). Move the chicken onto a clean plate. Lift the vegetables onto the same plate with a slotted spoon. Ladle 1 cup of liquid from the pot into a heatproof measuring cup. Pour the remaining liquid into a separate heatproof bowl to cool before discarding.

10). Pour the saved 1 cup liquid back into the Instant Pot. Select Sauté / Low. Add the cream cheese and stir until smooth. Add the heavy cream and warm gently for 3 minutes.

11). Press Cancel. Stir in the Parmesan a little at a time until melted.

12). Cut the chicken into bite-size pieces on the clean plate. Put the chicken, vegetables, and plate juices back into the Instant Pot with the sauce. Stir well. Use Sauté / Low to warm through if needed.

13). Divide the finished food and sauce evenly among 7 shallow containers. Each container is 1 serving. Follow the storage directions below.

Check doneness: Chicken must reach 165°F in the thickest parts. Use a food thermometer; cooking time alone is not a doneness check.
Store your 7 meals: Refrigerate in shallow containers within 2 hours (1 hour if the room is above 90°F). Keep the fridge at 40°F or colder. Keep the first 3 portions in the fridge and freeze the other 4 on cooking day. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3–4 days. Thaw frozen portions in the fridge.
Reheat: Heat each meal to 165°F throughout. In the microwave, use a vented cover, stir halfway through, let it stand briefly, and check several spots with a thermometer. Add a little water if needed.
Recipe developed for this collection; not kitchen-tested. Food safety: safe cooking temperatures, leftover storage, reheating. Cooker guidance: Instant Pot.

Steps:
Buffalo Shredded Chicken
High pressure: 12 minutes. Wait after cooking: 10 minutes with the lid closed, then release the remaining steam as explained below.
Approximate total time: 50–65 min. All meat must be thawed.
Use a 6- or 8-quart Instant Pot. Stay below the line marked PC MAX inside the metal pot, about two-thirds full. The amounts in the steps are for one full batch. If the full recipe will not fit, cook two half batches. Use half of each amount listed in the steps, except the broth added before pressure cooking: use 2¼ cups of that broth in EACH batch. Keep the pressure-cooking time the same. Buy extra broth for two batches. A 3-quart pot is too small for these directions.
Never fill above the PC MAX line. Follow your model manual for its minimum liquid and its steam-release controls.
In these directions, "pot" means the removable metal pot inside the Instant Pot. Keep the lid off until a step tells you to close it. Have a food thermometer, a large spoon, tongs, and a clean plate or bowl ready. Use thawed meat. Chop or slice the ingredients as listed before turning on the pot.

1). Put broth and seasonings into the pot
Pour 2¼ cups chicken broth into the Instant Pot. Stir in 2 teaspoons garlic powder, 2 teaspoons onion powder, and ½ teaspoon black pepper. No extra salt is needed because the bottled hot sauce is salty.

2). Add the raw chicken
Add all 4 pounds of thawed chicken thighs to the Instant Pot. Unfold any rolled-up pieces and arrange them in loose layers below the pressure-cooking maximum line. Keep the bottled hot sauce, butter, and cream cheese out of the pot for now.

3). Close the lid
Put the lid on the Instant Pot and turn it until it locks. If your model has a Sealing/Venting switch, move it to Sealing. Follow your model's instructions if its controls look different.

4). Cook on High pressure for 12 minutes
Press Pressure Cook. Choose High pressure and set the cooking time to 12 minutes. Press Start if your model requires it. The pot may take 15–25 minutes to build pressure before the cooking countdown starts.

5). Wait 10 minutes after cooking ends
When pressure cooking finishes, start a separate 10-minute timer. Leave the lid closed and do not touch the steam-release control during this wait. This lets some pressure drop by itself.

6). Let the remaining steam out
After the waiting time, use your model's steam-release control to release the remaining pressure. Keep your face and hands away from the steam. If liquid sprays out, stop releasing steam and wait longer before trying again.

7). Open the lid
Wait until steam stops coming out and the small pressure pin has dropped. Unlock the lid and lift it so the steam goes away from your face. Never force the lid open.

8). Check that the chicken is cooked
Put a food thermometer into the middle of several of the thickest chicken pieces. Each must read at least 165°F. If any piece is cooler, leave the lid off, select Sauté, and let the chicken cook in the bubbling broth. Check again until the thick pieces reach 165°F.

9). Move the cooked chicken to a clean plate
Use tongs to lift every chicken piece from the Instant Pot onto a clean plate. Leave the cooking liquid in the pot.

10). Shred the chicken on the plate
Use two forks to pull the chicken into small strips. Keep the shredded chicken and any juices on the plate. They will go into the finished Buffalo sauce later.

11). Save ½ cup of the cooking liquid
Use a ladle to put exactly ½ cup of hot liquid from the Instant Pot into a heatproof measuring cup. Set the cup aside.

12). Remove the extra liquid
Ladle all the remaining liquid from the Instant Pot into a separate heatproof bowl. Set that bowl aside to cool before discarding the extra liquid.

13). Start the Buffalo sauce
Pour the saved ½ cup cooking liquid back into the Instant Pot. Leave the lid off and select Sauté / Low. Add 4 tablespoons butter and the entire 8-ounce block of cream cheese, cut into small cubes.

14). Melt the butter and cream cheese
Stir or whisk the mixture in the Instant Pot for about 3–5 minutes, until the butter and cream cheese have melted and the sauce is smooth.

15). Add the bottled hot sauce
Pour 1 cup of plain cayenne pepper hot sauce into the Instant Pot. Stir it into the melted butter and cream cheese. Press Cancel.

16). Put the shredded chicken into the Buffalo sauce
Move all the shredded chicken from the plate into the Instant Pot with the Buffalo sauce. Pour in any juices left on the plate. Stir until the sauce coats all the chicken.

17). Warm the chicken and sauce together
Check the chicken with a food thermometer. If it has cooled below 165°F, select Sauté / Low and stir gently until the chicken and sauce reach 165°F. Avoid a hard boil. Press Cancel when finished.

18). Fill seven containers
Put out seven shallow food containers. Divide the cooked meat and all its sauce evenly among them. One container is one meal. Follow the cooling and storage directions below right away.

Food safety
All chicken must reach 165°F (74°C). Check several thick pieces; shredding and sauce color cannot replace a thermometer.
Wash hands and tools after handling raw meat; keep cooked food off raw-meat plates. Do not rinse raw poultry.

Meal-prep storage
Divide promptly into shallow containers; refrigerate within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C). Do not leave the full pot out to cool. Keep the fridge at 40°F / 4°C or colder. Keep portions 1-3 refrigerated and freeze portions 4-7 on cooking day. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3-4 days. Freeze at 0°F / -18°C or colder.

Freezing and reheating
Freeze chicken and sauce together; best texture within 2 months. Thaw a portion overnight in the refrigerator. Reheat covered on medium-low heat or in a microwave, stirring often to recombine the sauce, until 165°F throughout.
Reheat to 165°F / 74°C throughout. In a microwave, cover with a vented lid, stir or turn, let stand briefly and check several spots with a thermometer. Do not thaw on the counter or use Keep Warm to reheat. Freezer times above are quality targets.

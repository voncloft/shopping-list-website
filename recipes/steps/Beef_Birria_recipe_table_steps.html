Beef Birria
High pressure 60 minutes; wait 15 minutes. Allow 20 minutes to soften the dried peppers.
Use a 6- or 8-quart Instant Pot. Before closing the lid, keep the food and liquid below the PC MAX / 2/3 line. This full batch is too large for a 3-quart pot. Follow your model manual if it requires more liquid. Never force an overfilled batch into the cooker.
Use thawed meat. Cut and measure the ingredients as listed before starting. In these steps, "pot" means the removable metal pot inside the Instant Pot. Keep the lid off except during the pressure-cooking steps. Wash hands and tools after handling raw meat; use a clean plate for cooked meat.
Allow extra time for preparation, pressure to build (about 15–25 minutes), and finishing the sauce.

1). Put the stemmed, seeded dried peppers in a heatproof bowl. Bring 2 cups water to a boil in a kettle or saucepan. Pour it over the peppers. Hold them under the water with a small plate. Soak for 15 minutes.

2). Drain the peppers and discard the soaking water. Let the peppers cool until they are no longer steaming.

3). Put the softened peppers, 1 cup cool beef broth, onion, garlic, tomatoes with their juice, vinegar, cumin, oregano, cloves, and salt in a blender. Blend until smooth. Work in batches if the blender is small; do not seal hot liquid in a blender.

4). Pour the remaining 2 1/4 cups beef broth into the Instant Pot. Add the beef, cinnamon stick, and bay leaves. Pour the blended pepper mixture over the beef. Leave it on top; do not stir it against the bottom.

5). Lock the lid. Set the steam control to Sealing if your model has that switch. Select Pressure Cook / High for 60 minutes. Press Start if needed. The cooking countdown begins after pressure builds.

6). When cooking ends, leave the lid closed for 15 minutes. Then use your model's steam-release control. Keep your hands and face away from the steam. If liquid sprays, stop releasing and wait longer.

7). Wait until all steam stops and the pressure pin drops. Open the lid away from your face. Never force the lid open.

8). Check a beef piece with a fork. It should pull apart easily. If it is tough, select Sauté / Low and simmer uncovered for 15–30 more minutes. Stir often; add up to 1/2 cup water if needed to keep the sauce from sticking.

9). Press Cancel. Remove and discard both bay leaves and the cinnamon stick.

10). Move the cooked meat onto a clean plate. Pull it apart with two forks. Leave the cooking liquid in the Instant Pot.

11). Put the shredded beef and plate juices back into the Instant Pot with all the broth. Stir in the lime juice and cilantro.

12). Divide the finished food and sauce evenly among 7 shallow containers. Each container is 1 serving. Follow the storage directions below.

Check doneness: Whole-cut beef and pork must reach at least 145°F and rest 3 minutes before eating. These shoulder and chuck recipes need longer cooking until fork-tender; do not stop at the minimum temperature if the meat is still tough.
Store your 7 meals: Refrigerate in shallow containers within 2 hours (1 hour if the room is above 90°F). Keep the fridge at 40°F or colder. Keep the first 3 portions in the fridge and freeze the other 4 on cooking day. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3–4 days. Thaw frozen portions in the fridge.
Reheat: Heat each meal to 165°F throughout. In the microwave, use a vented cover, stir halfway through, let it stand briefly, and check several spots with a thermometer. Add a little water if needed.
Recipe developed for this collection; not kitchen-tested. Food safety: safe cooking temperatures, leftover storage, reheating. Cooker guidance: Instant Pot.

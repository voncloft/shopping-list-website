Extract into your existing shopping_list website root and overwrite the three
matching files, then hard-refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R. No SQL import is needed.

Pantry inventory keeps Item, Department, Quantity at home, and Status together
within the page width. Long names and messages wrap. Quantity fields stay visible
and editable. The table uses normal up/down page scrolling, with no separate
scrolling box or sideways scrolling needed to reach the quantity fields.

Your chosen theme, automatic per-row saves and Enter-to-save are preserved.
The final shopping list and other editing pages are unchanged by this patch.

Checked locally in Firefox and Chromium at widths 360–1333px, including large
26px theme text and 18px theme padding, long product names and error messages,
real test-database quantity saves, failed-save retry and the no-JavaScript Save
button. No production database or server files have been changed.

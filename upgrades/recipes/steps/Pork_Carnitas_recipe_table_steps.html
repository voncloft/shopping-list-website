Pork Carnitas
High pressure: 60 minutes. Natural release: 20 minutes, then carefully vent the remainder.
Approximate total time: 1 hr 45 min–2 hr 10 min. All meat must be thawed.
Use a 6- or 8-quart pot. If the load exceeds PC MAX, split the ingredients into two batches and use a full 540 ml broth in EACH batch, with the same pressure time. Buy extra broth for that split-batch option. Never exceed PC MAX / 2/3. Follow your model manual for its liquid minimum and release controls. Never force the lid; open only after the float drops.

1). Load the pot
Add broth, orange juice and lime juice to the inner pot. Add onion, garlic, cumin, oregano, salt, pepper and bay leaves. Add thawed pork and turn to coat. Do not exceed PC MAX. No browning step is needed.

2). Pressure cook
Lock the lid, set Sealing if required, and cook on High pressure for 60 minutes. Expect about 15–25 minutes for the pot to pressurize.

3). Release gently
Let pressure release naturally for 20 minutes, then carefully vent the remainder. Wait for the float to drop before opening. The pork should separate with a fork. If it resists, cook in the liquid for another 10 minutes on High, then naturally release for 10 minutes before venting.

4). Shred and moisten
Remove bay leaves and shred pork on a clean tray. Toss with 1½ cups (360 ml) of the cooking juices. Keep more juice to loosen it if needed. Remove visible excess fat if you prefer; this changes the calorie estimate.

5). Crisp, if serving now
Use two broiler-safe metal sheet pans, with no parchment. Spread the pork in a thin layer and drizzle with the measured tablespoon of olive oil, split across both pans. Broil one pan at a time about 6 inches from the element for 4–8 minutes, watching constantly, until the edges brown. Follow your oven’s broiler instructions.

6). Portion for the week
Divide the pork and its retained juices into seven equal portions. For better meal-prep texture, store it before crisping and reserve the oil: divide the measured 14 g tablespoon across the seven portions (2 g oil each). Reheat to 165°F first, then briefly crisp.

Food safety
Whole-cut pork has a minimum safe temperature of 145°F (63°C) with a 3-minute rest. Shoulder must also be cooked until fork-tender to make carnitas.
Wash hands and tools after handling raw meat; keep cooked food off raw-meat plates. Do not rinse raw poultry.

Meal-prep storage
Divide promptly into shallow containers; refrigerate within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C). Do not leave the full pot out to cool. Keep the fridge at 40°F / 4°C or colder. Keep portions 1-3 refrigerated and freeze portions 4-7 on cooking day. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3-4 days. Freeze at 0°F / -18°C or colder.

Freezing and reheating
Freeze the shredded pork with juice, preferably before crisping. Best texture within 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator. Reheat covered with a splash of water until 165°F throughout; then crisp in a skillet or under the broiler.
Reheat to 165°F / 74°C throughout. In a microwave, cover with a vented lid, stir or turn, let stand briefly and check several spots with a thermometer. Do not thaw on the counter or use Keep Warm to reheat. Freezer times above are quality targets.

Recipe nutrition estimate
About 660 kcal and 46 g protein per one-seventh of the finished recipe, including sauce, excluding sides. Allow roughly 20% variation in calories and 15% in protein, sometimes more for fatty meat.

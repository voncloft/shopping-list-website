Italian Meatballs in Marinara
High pressure: 10 minutes. Natural release: 10 minutes, then carefully vent the remainder.
Approximate total time: 65–85 min (8-qt). All meat must be thawed.
Use an 8-quart pot for the full batch; use two batches of 21 meatballs in a 6-quart pot, with 540 ml water for EACH batch. Never exceed PC MAX / 2/3. Follow your model manual for its liquid minimum and release controls. Never force the lid; open only after the float drops.

1). Make the mixture
Soak breadcrumbs in milk for 5 minutes. Mix in eggs, Parmesan, garlic, parsley, Italian seasoning, salt and pepper. Add thawed ground beef and pork and mix gently just until combined; do not taste the raw mixture.

2). Shape 42 meatballs
Divide the mixture into 42 equal balls, about 50–55 g each and roughly 1¾ inches (4.5 cm) across. Each finished serving gets 6 meatballs plus one-seventh of the sauce. Keep raw meatballs refrigerated until the pot is ready.

3). Choose your batch size
Use an 8-quart pot for the full batch. For a 6-quart pot, cook 21 meatballs at a time; refrigerate the second half while the first cooks. Pour a full 540 ml water into the pot for each batch. Gently nestle meatballs in loose layers in the water without packing them down. Do not exceed PC MAX. No marinara goes in yet.

4). Pressure and release
Lock the lid, set Sealing if required, and cook on High pressure for 10 minutes. Allow about 15–25 minutes to pressurize. Naturally release for 10 minutes, then vent remaining pressure carefully. Open only after the float drops.

5). Check the centers
Insert a thermometer into the centers of several meatballs, including the largest and those from different layers. Each must reach at least 160°F (71°C). If any are below this, simmer them in the cooking liquid until 160°F. Lift out gently with a slotted spoon. For a second batch, remove the first liquid and start with fresh 540 ml water; keep the first cooked batch hot at 140°F (60°C) or above in a separate covered saucepan.

6). Finish in marinara
Remove the pressure-cooking liquid. Add both jars of marinara to the empty pot on Sauté / Low (or use a large saucepan if everything will not fit). Stir and warm until bubbling gently. Fold in all cooked meatballs and simmer uncovered for 5–10 minutes. If the meatballs have cooled, bring their centers to 165°F. Divide into seven portions of 6 meatballs and equal sauce.

Food safety
Ground beef and pork must reach 160°F (71°C) in the center of each meatball. Do not use the 145°F whole-cut-meat rule. Reheated cooked meatballs need 165°F (74°C).
Wash hands and tools after handling raw meat; keep cooked food off raw-meat plates. Do not rinse raw poultry.

Meal-prep storage
Divide promptly into shallow containers; refrigerate within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C). Do not leave the full pot out to cool. Keep the fridge at 40°F / 4°C or colder. Keep portions 1-3 refrigerated and freeze portions 4-7 on cooking day. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3-4 days. Freeze at 0°F / -18°C or colder.

Freezing and reheating
Freeze six meatballs per portion in their share of sauce; best texture within 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator. Reheat covered in a saucepan or microwave, turning gently, until the centers of several meatballs reach 165°F.
Reheat to 165°F / 74°C throughout. In a microwave, cover with a vented lid, stir or turn, let stand briefly and check several spots with a thermometer. Do not thaw on the counter or use Keep Warm to reheat. Freezer times above are quality targets.

Recipe nutrition estimate
About 840 kcal and 59 g protein per one-seventh of the finished recipe, including sauce, excluding sides. Allow roughly 20% variation in calories and 15% in protein, sometimes more for fatty meat. These recipe estimates use generic ingredients; your app calculates from its item records, which can differ. Assumes 85%-lean beef, regular ground pork and marinara at about 60 kcal per 100 g. Counts all meat fat before cooking; discarding the cooking liquid will usually reduce actual calories. Six meatballs plus sauce is one serving.
Optional serving suggestion: Cook 14 oz (397 g) dry pasta separately for the batch: 2 oz (57 g) dry per serving, about +200 kcal and +7 g protein. That makes roughly 1040 kcal per pasta-and-meatball serving. Extra Parmesan is additional.

Chipotle Beef Barbacoa
High pressure: 60 minutes. Wait after cooking: 20 minutes with the lid closed, then release the remaining steam as explained below.
Approximate total time: 1 hr 50 min–2 hr 15 min. All meat must be thawed.
Use a 6- or 8-quart Instant Pot. Stay below the line marked PC MAX inside the metal pot, about two-thirds full. The amounts in the steps are for one full batch. If the full recipe will not fit, cook two half batches. Use half of each amount listed in the steps, except the broth added before pressure cooking: use 2¼ cups of that broth in EACH batch. Keep the pressure-cooking time the same. Buy extra broth for two batches. A 3-quart pot is too small for these directions.
Never fill above the PC MAX line. Follow your model manual for its minimum liquid and its steam-release controls.
In these directions, "pot" means the removable metal pot inside the Instant Pot. Keep the lid off until a step tells you to close it. Have a food thermometer, a large spoon, tongs, and a clean plate or bowl ready. Use thawed meat. Chop or slice the ingredients as listed before turning on the pot.

1). Get the beef ready
Use 4 pounds of thawed, trimmed beef chuck. Cut it into chunks about 3 inches across. Pat the meat dry with paper towels. Sprinkle it with 1 teaspoon salt and 1 teaspoon black pepper.

2). Brown the first batch of beef
Leave the lid off. Select Sauté / High and add 1 tablespoon olive oil to the Instant Pot. Add only enough beef to cover the bottom in one layer. Cook about 2 minutes on each broad side, until browned.

3). Put the browned beef on a plate
Move the browned pieces from the pot to a plate. Brown the remaining beef the same way, in two or three batches total. Move every batch to the plate. The beef is still raw inside at this point.

4). Cook the onion in the empty pot
With all the beef on the plate, add the diced onion to the same Instant Pot. Leave the oil and browned bits in the pot. Stir and cook the onion for 3 minutes.

5). Add the garlic
Add the 6 finely chopped garlic cloves to the onion in the pot. Stir for 30 seconds.

6). Add broth and loosen the browned bits
Press Cancel. Pour 2¼ cups beef broth into the Instant Pot. Use a wooden spoon to scrape the stuck browned bits off the bottom. Keep scraping until the bottom feels smooth.

7). Add the seasonings to the broth
Stir in ¼ cup apple cider vinegar, the 3 chopped chipotle peppers, 2 tablespoons adobo sauce, 1 tablespoon cumin, 2 teaspoons oregano, and 2 bay leaves.

8). Put the beef back into the pot
Move all the beef from the plate into the broth in the Instant Pot. Pour in any meat juices left on the plate. Make sure the food and liquid are below the pressure-cooking maximum line inside the pot.

9). Close the lid
Put the lid on the Instant Pot and turn it until it locks. If your model has a Sealing/Venting switch, move it to Sealing. Follow your model's instructions if its controls look different.

10). Cook on High pressure for 60 minutes
Press Pressure Cook. Choose High pressure and set the cooking time to 60 minutes. Press Start if your model requires it. The pot may take 15–25 minutes to build pressure before the cooking countdown starts.

11). Wait 20 minutes after cooking ends
When pressure cooking finishes, start a separate 20-minute timer. Leave the lid closed and do not touch the steam-release control during this wait. This lets some pressure drop by itself.

12). Let the remaining steam out
After the waiting time, use your model's steam-release control to release the remaining pressure. Keep your face and hands away from the steam. If liquid sprays out, stop releasing steam and wait longer before trying again.

13). Open the lid
Wait until steam stops coming out and the small pressure pin has dropped. Unlock the lid and lift it so the steam goes away from your face. Never force the lid open.

14). Check whether the beef pulls apart
Try pulling a chunk apart with two forks. It should come apart easily. If it is still firm, leave it in the broth, pressure cook for another 10 minutes on High, wait 10 minutes, then release the remaining steam and open the lid as described above. Check again.

15). Remove the bay leaves and lift out the beef
Find and throw away both bay leaves. Use tongs to move the cooked beef to a clean tray. Leave the cooking liquid in the Instant Pot. Use a clean tray, not the unwashed plate that held partly raw beef.

16). Pull the beef into shreds
Use two forks to pull the beef on the tray into small strips. Leave the shredded beef on the tray for now.

17). Cook down the liquid in the pot
Leave the lid off. Select Sauté and let the cooking liquid bubble for 10–15 minutes. Stir often. Some water will evaporate, making the flavor stronger. The liquid will still be thin and juicy.

18). Add the lime juice
Press Cancel. Stir ¼ cup lime juice into the cooking liquid in the Instant Pot.

19). Put the shredded beef into the juice
Move the shredded beef from the tray back into the Instant Pot. Add any juices left on the tray. Stir so the lime-flavored cooking juice coats all the meat.

20). Fill seven containers
Put out seven shallow food containers. Divide the cooked meat and all its sauce evenly among them. One container is one meal. Follow the cooling and storage directions below right away.

Food safety
Whole-cut beef has a minimum safe temperature of 145°F (63°C) followed by a 3-minute rest. Chuck needs much longer cooking to shred; check both temperature and fork-tenderness.
Wash hands and tools after handling raw meat; keep cooked food off raw-meat plates. Do not rinse raw poultry.

Meal-prep storage
Divide promptly into shallow containers; refrigerate within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C). Do not leave the full pot out to cool. Keep the fridge at 40°F / 4°C or colder. Keep portions 1-3 refrigerated and freeze portions 4-7 on cooking day. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3-4 days. Freeze at 0°F / -18°C or colder.

Freezing and reheating
Freeze meat with its juices. Best texture within 3 months. Thaw one portion overnight in the refrigerator; reheat covered with 1–2 tbsp water or broth, stirring to break up cold pockets.
Reheat to 165°F / 74°C throughout. In a microwave, cover with a vented lid, stir or turn, let stand briefly and check several spots with a thermometer. Do not thaw on the counter or use Keep Warm to reheat. Freezer times above are quality targets.

Recipe nutrition estimate
About 620 kcal and 51 g protein per one-seventh of the finished recipe, including sauce, excluding sides. Allow roughly 20% variation in calories and 15% in protein, sometimes more for fatty meat. These recipe estimates use generic ingredients; your app calculates from its item records, which can differ. The estimate counts all beef fat, oil and juices as eaten. Trimming or discarding rendered fat can lower calories substantially.
Optional serving suggestion: For a rice bowl, add 1 cup cooked rice per serving (7 cups for the batch): about +205 kcal and +4 g protein. With the barbacoa, that is roughly 825 kcal per bowl. Your keto tortillas also work; count their package-label nutrition separately.

Creamy Garlic Parmesan Chicken
High pressure: 10 minutes. Natural release: 10 minutes, then carefully vent the remainder.
Approximate total time: 55–75 min. All meat must be thawed.
Use a 6- or 8-quart pot. If the load exceeds PC MAX, split the ingredients into two batches and use a full 540 ml broth in EACH batch, with the same pressure time. Buy extra broth for that split-batch option. Never exceed PC MAX / 2/3. Follow your model manual for its liquid minimum and release controls. Never force the lid; open only after the float drops.

1). Sauté the aromatics
Select Sauté / Normal. Add oil and onion; cook 4 minutes. Add garlic and stir for 30 seconds. Press Cancel. Add the full 540 ml broth and scrape the bottom clean.

2). Load the chicken
Stir in Italian seasoning, salt and pepper. Add the thawed thighs in loose, overlapping layers, unfolding any tightly rolled pieces. Do not pack into one solid block. Keep below PC MAX. Leave all dairy, spinach and lemon out for now.

3). Pressure cook
Lock the lid, set Sealing if needed, and cook on High pressure for 10 minutes. Allow about 15–25 minutes to reach pressure.

4). Release and verify
Naturally release for 10 minutes, then carefully vent the remainder. Once the float drops, open and check several of the thickest thighs: all must reach 165°F (74°C). If any are below that, simmer them in the liquid on Sauté until they reach 165°F.

5). Make the sauce
Move chicken to a clean plate. Measure 1 cup (240 ml) of the hot cooking liquid back into the inner pot; remove the rest. Set Sauté / Low. Whisk cream cheese into the reserved liquid until smooth, then add heavy cream. Heat gently for 3–5 minutes. Press Cancel and stir in Parmesan a little at a time. Avoid a hard boil after adding cheese.

6). Finish and portion
Stir in spinach until wilted, then lemon juice. Slice or shred the chicken and return it with its plate juices. Mix and divide into seven equal portions, including all sauce. If the chicken has cooled below 165°F during finishing, heat the mixture gently back to 165°F before serving.

Food safety
Chicken must reach 165°F (74°C) in its thickest parts. Check multiple pieces with a food thermometer; appearance and pressure time alone do not confirm doneness.
Wash hands and tools after handling raw meat; keep cooked food off raw-meat plates. Do not rinse raw poultry.

Meal-prep storage
Divide promptly into shallow containers; refrigerate within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C). Do not leave the full pot out to cool. Keep the fridge at 40°F / 4°C or colder. Keep portions 1-3 refrigerated and freeze portions 4-7 on cooking day. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3-4 days. Freeze at 0°F / -18°C or colder.

Freezing and reheating
Freeze the fully cooked chicken and sauce together; best texture within 2 months. Cream sauce may separate after freezing. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator, then reheat gently while stirring; a splash of water helps bring the sauce together.
Reheat to 165°F / 74°C throughout. In a microwave, cover with a vented lid, stir or turn, let stand briefly and check several spots with a thermometer. Do not thaw on the counter or use Keep Warm to reheat. Freezer times above are quality targets.

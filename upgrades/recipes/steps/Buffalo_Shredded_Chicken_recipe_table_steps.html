Buffalo Shredded Chicken
High pressure: 12 minutes. Natural release: 10 minutes, then carefully vent the remainder.
Approximate total time: 50–65 min. All meat must be thawed.
Use a 6- or 8-quart pot. If the load exceeds PC MAX, split the ingredients into two batches and use a full 540 ml broth in EACH batch, with the same pressure time. Buy extra broth for that split-batch option. Never exceed PC MAX / 2/3. Follow your model manual for its liquid minimum and release controls. Never force the lid; open only after the float drops.

1). Load the chicken
Pour the full 540 ml broth into the pot. Stir in garlic powder, onion powder and pepper. Add the thawed, unfolded thighs in loose layers below PC MAX. Keep hot sauce, butter and cream cheese out until after pressure cooking. No extra salt is included because hot sauce is salty.

2). Pressure cook
Lock the lid and set Sealing if required. Cook on High pressure for 12 minutes. Allow about 15–25 minutes to reach pressure.

3). Release and check
Naturally release for 10 minutes, then carefully vent the rest. Open after the float drops. Check several of the thickest thighs with a thermometer: they must reach 165°F (74°C). If needed, simmer in the cooking liquid on Sauté until they do.

4). Shred and reserve liquid
Move chicken to a clean plate and shred with two forks. Measure ½ cup (120 ml) cooking liquid back into the pot; remove the rest. Keep the shredded chicken and its plate juices.

5). Make the Buffalo sauce
Select Sauté / Low. Whisk butter and cream cheese into the reserved liquid until smooth, about 3–5 minutes. Stir in the full cup of hot sauce. Turn off the heat. Fold in shredded chicken and its juices.

6). Portion
Divide the chicken and sauce into seven equal portions. For sandwiches or wraps, pack bread or tortillas separately. If the chicken has cooled below 165°F during finishing, gently heat the combined dish back to 165°F before serving.

Food safety
All chicken must reach 165°F (74°C). Check several thick pieces; shredding and sauce color cannot replace a thermometer.
Wash hands and tools after handling raw meat; keep cooked food off raw-meat plates. Do not rinse raw poultry.

Meal-prep storage
Divide promptly into shallow containers; refrigerate within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C). Do not leave the full pot out to cool. Keep the fridge at 40°F / 4°C or colder. Keep portions 1-3 refrigerated and freeze portions 4-7 on cooking day. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3-4 days. Freeze at 0°F / -18°C or colder.

Freezing and reheating
Freeze chicken and sauce together; best texture within 2 months. Thaw a portion overnight in the refrigerator. Reheat covered on medium-low heat or in a microwave, stirring often to recombine the sauce, until 165°F throughout.
Reheat to 165°F / 74°C throughout. In a microwave, cover with a vented lid, stir or turn, let stand briefly and check several spots with a thermometer. Do not thaw on the counter or use Keep Warm to reheat. Freezer times above are quality targets.

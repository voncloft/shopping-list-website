Steps:
Beef Stroganoff
High pressure: 35 minutes. Wait after cooking: 15 minutes with the lid closed, then release the remaining steam as explained below.
Approximate total time: 1 hr 35 min–1 hr 55 min. All meat must be thawed.
Use a 6- or 8-quart Instant Pot. Stay below the line marked PC MAX inside the metal pot, about two-thirds full. The amounts in the steps are for one full batch. If the full recipe will not fit, cook two half batches. Use half of each amount listed in the steps, except the broth added before pressure cooking: use 2¼ cups of that broth in EACH batch. Keep the pressure-cooking time the same. Buy extra broth for two batches. A 3-quart pot is too small for these directions.
Never fill above the PC MAX line. Follow your model manual for its minimum liquid and its steam-release controls.
In these directions, "pot" means the removable metal pot inside the Instant Pot. Keep the lid off until a step tells you to close it. Have a food thermometer, a large spoon, tongs, and a clean plate or bowl ready. Use thawed meat. Chop or slice the ingredients as listed before turning on the pot.

1). Get the beef ready
Use 4 pounds of thawed, trimmed beef chuck. Cut it into chunks about 1½ inches across. Pat the beef dry with paper towels. Sprinkle it with 1 teaspoon salt and 1 teaspoon black pepper.

2). Cook half the mushrooms
Leave the lid off. Select Sauté / High. Melt 2 tablespoons butter in the Instant Pot. Add half of the sliced mushrooms. Stir and cook for 5–6 minutes, until most of the water they release has cooked away.

3). Cook the remaining mushrooms
Move the first mushrooms to a clean bowl. Cook the other half of the mushrooms in the same pot for 5–6 minutes, then put them in that bowl too. Keep all the mushrooms in the bowl until after the beef has pressure cooked.

4). Brown the first batch of beef
Add 1 tablespoon olive oil to the Instant Pot. Add about one-third of the beef in a single layer. Cook about 2 minutes on each broad side, until browned. Move these pieces to a plate.

5). Brown the rest of the beef
Brown the remaining beef in two more batches the same way. Put each batch on the plate. All the beef should now be on the plate, outside the pot. It is still raw in the middle.

6). Cook the onion in the pot
With the beef on the plate and the mushrooms in their bowl, add the diced onion to the Instant Pot. Leave the oil and browned bits in the pot. Stir and cook the onion for 3 minutes.

7). Add the garlic
Add the 4 finely chopped garlic cloves to the onion in the Instant Pot. Stir for 30 seconds.

8). Add broth and loosen the browned bits
Press Cancel. Pour 2¼ cups beef broth into the Instant Pot. Scrape the bottom with a wooden spoon until all the stuck browned bits come loose and the bottom feels smooth.

9). Add Worcestershire and mustard
Stir 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce and 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard into the broth in the Instant Pot.

10). Put the beef back into the pot
Move all the browned beef from the plate into the broth in the Instant Pot. Pour in any juices left on the plate. Stay below the pressure-cooking maximum line. Keep the mushrooms, cornstarch, sour cream, and any noodles out for now.

11). Close the lid
Put the lid on the Instant Pot and turn it until it locks. If your model has a Sealing/Venting switch, move it to Sealing. Follow your model's instructions if its controls look different.

12). Cook on High pressure for 35 minutes
Press Pressure Cook. Choose High pressure and set the cooking time to 35 minutes. Press Start if your model requires it. The pot may take 15–25 minutes to build pressure before the cooking countdown starts.

13). Wait 15 minutes after cooking ends
When pressure cooking finishes, start a separate 15-minute timer. Leave the lid closed and do not touch the steam-release control during this wait. This lets some pressure drop by itself.

14). Let the remaining steam out
After the waiting time, use your model's steam-release control to release the remaining pressure. Keep your face and hands away from the steam. If liquid sprays out, stop releasing steam and wait longer before trying again.

15). Open the lid
Wait until steam stops coming out and the small pressure pin has dropped. Unlock the lid and lift it so the steam goes away from your face. Never force the lid open.

16). Check that the beef is tender
Try cutting a beef chunk with the side of a fork. It should break apart easily. If it is tough, leave it in the broth, pressure cook for another 5–10 minutes on High, wait 10 minutes, then release the remaining steam and open the lid. Check again.

17). Put the mushrooms back into the pot
Pour the cooked mushrooms and any liquid in their bowl into the Instant Pot with the beef. Stir.

18). Mix cornstarch with cold water
In a small bowl, mix 3 tablespoons cornstarch with 3 tablespoons cold water. Stir with a fork until there are no dry lumps. Keep this bowl beside the pot.

19). Bring the beef sauce to a gentle bubble
Leave the lid off. Select Sauté / Normal. Stir the beef and mushrooms until the liquid in the Instant Pot starts bubbling gently.

20). Thicken the sauce
Stir the cornstarch mixture again. Slowly pour it into the bubbling liquid in the Instant Pot while stirring. Cook and stir for 2–3 minutes, until the sauce thickens.

21). Turn off the heat
Press Cancel and wait until the sauce stops bubbling. Leave the beef, mushrooms, and sauce in the Instant Pot.

22). Warm the sour cream in a separate bowl
Put 1½ cups sour cream into a bowl. Scoop ½ cup of warm stroganoff gravy from the Instant Pot. Slowly stir that gravy into the sour cream. This warms the sour cream so it mixes into the pot more smoothly.

23). Pour the sour-cream mixture into the pot
Pour the warmed sour-cream mixture from the bowl into the Instant Pot with the beef and mushrooms. Stir gently until the sauce is creamy and evenly mixed. If it needs warming, use Sauté / Low briefly. Do not let it boil hard.

24). Fill seven containers
Divide the beef, mushrooms, and all the creamy gravy evenly among seven shallow containers. Keep any noodles separate. Follow the cooling and storage directions below right away.

Food safety
Whole-cut beef must reach at least 145°F (63°C) with a 3-minute rest. Chuck should also be fork-tender; tough beef needs more cooking even when it is already safe.
Wash hands and tools after handling raw meat; keep cooked food off raw-meat plates. Do not rinse raw poultry.

Meal-prep storage
Divide promptly into shallow containers; refrigerate within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C). Do not leave the full pot out to cool. Keep the fridge at 40°F / 4°C or colder. Keep portions 1-3 refrigerated and freeze portions 4-7 on cooking day. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3-4 days. Freeze at 0°F / -18°C or colder.

Freezing and reheating
Freeze the beef and sauce without noodles; best texture within 2 months. Sour cream may become a little grainy after thawing. Thaw in the refrigerator and reheat gently, stirring, until 165°F. Add 1–2 tbsp water if the gravy is too thick.
Reheat to 165°F / 74°C throughout. In a microwave, cover with a vented lid, stir or turn, let stand briefly and check several spots with a thermometer. Do not thaw on the counter or use Keep Warm to reheat. Freezer times above are quality targets.

Recipe nutrition estimate
About 780 kcal and 55 g protein per one-seventh of the finished recipe, including sauce, excluding sides. Allow roughly 20% variation in calories and 15% in protein, sometimes more for fatty meat. These recipe estimates use generic ingredients; your app calculates from its item records, which can differ. Base nutrition includes meat, mushrooms, gravy and sour cream; it excludes noodles. All cooking oil, butter and beef fat are counted.

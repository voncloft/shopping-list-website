Pork Chile Verde
High pressure: 45 minutes. Natural release: 15 minutes, then carefully vent the remainder.
Approximate total time: 1 hr 30 min–1 hr 50 min. All meat must be thawed.
Use a 6- or 8-quart pot. If the load exceeds PC MAX, split the ingredients into two batches and use a full 540 ml broth in EACH batch, with the same pressure time. Buy extra broth for that split-batch option. Never exceed PC MAX / 2/3. Follow your model manual for its liquid minimum and release controls. Never force the lid; open only after the float drops.

1). Brown the pork
Select Sauté / High and add the oil. Season thawed pork with the salt and pepper. Brown in three batches, about 2 minutes per broad side; transfer to a plate.

2). Deglaze
Cook onion in the pot for 3 minutes, then garlic for 30 seconds. Press Cancel. Add the full 540 ml broth and scrape every stuck browned bit from the bottom. Stir in cumin and oregano, then return pork and its juices. Keep below PC MAX; salsa and canned chiles stay out for now.

3). Pressure cook
Lock the lid, set Sealing if required, and cook on High pressure for 45 minutes. Allow about 15–25 minutes to come to pressure.

4). Release and check tenderness
Naturally release for 15 minutes, then carefully vent remaining pressure. Open only after the float drops. Pork should be fork-tender. If it is still chewy, pressure cook another 10 minutes in the liquid and naturally release for 10 minutes before venting.

5). Finish the green sauce
Transfer pork to a clean bowl. Select Sauté and simmer the cooking liquid uncovered for 8–15 minutes to reduce it somewhat. Stir in all salsa verde and undrained green chiles. Return pork; simmer 5–8 minutes, stirring, until the sauce is as thick as you like. Keep the lid off during this step.

6). Brighten and portion
Press Cancel. Stir in lime juice and cilantro. Keep the pork in chunks or gently shred it. Divide all meat and sauce evenly among seven shallow containers. Do not add avocado or avocado-based salsa.

Food safety
Whole-cut pork needs at least 145°F (63°C) with a 3-minute rest. Shoulder should also be fork-tender. Check salsa labels carefully if avoiding avocado for an allergy.
Wash hands and tools after handling raw meat; keep cooked food off raw-meat plates. Do not rinse raw poultry.

Meal-prep storage
Divide promptly into shallow containers; refrigerate within 2 hours (1 hour above 90°F / 32°C). Do not leave the full pot out to cool. Keep the fridge at 40°F / 4°C or colder. Keep portions 1-3 refrigerated and freeze portions 4-7 on cooking day. Use refrigerated leftovers within 3-4 days. Freeze at 0°F / -18°C or colder.

Freezing and reheating
Freeze pork and green sauce together; best texture within 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator. Reheat covered in a saucepan or microwave, stirring, until the pork and sauce reach 165°F throughout.
Reheat to 165°F / 74°C throughout. In a microwave, cover with a vented lid, stir or turn, let stand briefly and check several spots with a thermometer. Do not thaw on the counter or use Keep Warm to reheat. Freezer times above are quality targets.

Ingredients:
Makes 7 generous meal-prep servings.
4 lb (1.81 kg) boneless pork shoulder / Boston butt, weighed after removing skin and the thick exterior fat cap; cut into 2-inch (5 cm) cubes
1 tbsp (14 g) olive oil
1 large yellow onion (200 g), diced
6 garlic cloves (18 g), minced
2¼ cups (540 ml) low-sodium chicken broth
2 tsp ground cumin (4 g)
1 tsp dried oregano (1 g)
½ tsp fine salt (3 g) and ½ tsp ground black pepper (1 g)
24 oz (680 g) jarred tomatillo salsa verde, mild or medium, without avocado
Two 4-oz cans diced roasted green chiles (227 g total), undrained
¼ cup (60 ml) lime juice
½ cup loosely packed chopped cilantro (15 g)
Shopping note: two 16-oz salsa jars supply the 24 oz / 680 g needed; save the remainder.
Allergens: Check salsa and broth ingredient labels. Choose tomatillo salsa without avocado; recipes and brands vary.

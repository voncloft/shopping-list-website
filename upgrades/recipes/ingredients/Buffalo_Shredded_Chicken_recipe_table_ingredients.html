Ingredients:
Makes 7 generous meal-prep servings.
4 lb (1.81 kg) boneless, skinless chicken thighs, thawed and unfolded
2¼ cups (540 ml) low-sodium chicken broth
2 tsp garlic powder (6 g)
2 tsp onion powder (5 g)
½ tsp ground black pepper (1 g)
1 cup (240 ml) plain cayenne pepper hot sauce, such as original-style Louisiana hot sauce; not premixed buttery wing sauce
4 tbsp (56 g) unsalted butter
8 oz (227 g) full-fat block cream cheese, cubed
Allergens: Contains milk. Check hot sauce and broth labels.
